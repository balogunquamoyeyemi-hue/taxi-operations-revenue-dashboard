# TAXI OPERATIONS & REVENUE DASHBOARD

** OVERVIEW**
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of taxi operations, focusing on demand patterns, revenue    generation, pricing dynamics, and customer behavior.
It combines operationsl and financial metrics to help stakeholders understand when demand peaks, how revenue is distributed across locations, and how external factors like traffic and weather influence performance.

**OBJECTIVES**
- Analyze total trip volume and revenue performance
- Identify peak demand hours and weekly trends
- Evaluate revenue contribution by pickup zones
- Understand the impact of traffic and weather on revenue
- Examine customer behavior through trip purpose and payment methods
- Assess the effect of surge pricing on operations

**KEY INSIGHTS**
- A total of 1000 trips generated approximately $20k in revenue
- Average fare per trip stood at $16, indicating consistent pricing across trips
- Average trip distance was 5.50 miles, suggesting moderate ride lengths.
- 47% of trips occurred under surge pricing, highligting high-demand conditions.
- Revenue distribution varied across pickup zones, with certain areas contributiong significantly more.
- Peak demands was concentrated during specific hours of the day, reflecting commuter and activity patterns
- Traffic conditions influenced revenue, with heavier traffic often associated with higher fares.
- Weather conditions also impacted trips patterns and revenue generation.
- Payment methods showed a mix of card and cash usage, reflecting customer preferences.
